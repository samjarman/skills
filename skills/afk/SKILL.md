# AFK

Use the goal supplied with this invocation, or the goal already agreed in the conversation. I'm going AFK now. Keep working until the stated goal is met. Don't ask questions; make reasonable assumptions and proceed. Stop only when the work is done or you reach a guardrail or blocker you cannot resolve within your existing permissions. When you stop, summarize what you completed, any important assumptions, and anything still blocked.
