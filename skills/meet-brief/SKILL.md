# Meet the brief

Re-read the ticket for this unit of work, including its acceptance criteria. Identify the intended base branch and review this branch's diff from their merge base to see what changed. Did the work meet the brief? Explain why or why not, linking each requirement to evidence in the changes and calling out gaps or work outside the brief. Be clear about anything you couldn't verify. If the ticket or base branch cannot be identified, say what's missing rather than guessing.
